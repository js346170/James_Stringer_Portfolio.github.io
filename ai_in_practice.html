About the Course

AI in Practice: Applying AI is a professional certificate program offered by DelftX, the online learning initiative of Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands. The course focuses on the practical application of artificial intelligence in real-world professional and organizational contexts, moving beyond theory to examine how AI tools, systems, and workflows are actually implemented and managed.

Topics Covered

Practical AI Implementation AI in Organizations Machine Learning Applications AI Ethics & Responsible Use Data-Driven Decision Making AI Strategy

Relevance to My Work

AI Research & Interventions at DGS

This course directly informed the year-long AI literacy initiative at Delta Global School that forms the basis of my AP Annual Conference presentation. The practical implementation frameworks from the program shaped how I designed and measured the four classroom interventions — particularly around responsible AI use, structured workflows, and institutional AI strategy.

Socratic AI Tutor App

The course's emphasis on practical AI application — designing tools and workflows that genuinely augment human thinking rather than replace it — directly influenced the design philosophy behind the Socratic AI Tutor app. The app was built to operationalize responsible, structured AI use in a classroom context.
